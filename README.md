# Regional Sales Analysis

## Project Description

Regional Sales Analysis is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing **5 years of historical sales data across U.S. regions, states, products, customers, and sales channels**. The objective of the project is to identify sales trends, profitability patterns, regional performance, product contribution, and customer segments to support data-driven business decisions.

The project involved **data loading, preprocessing, feature engineering, exploratory data analysis (EDA), visualization, and interactive Power BI dashboard development**. Multiple datasets including sales, customers, products, regions, state-region mappings, and budgets were consolidated into a structured analytical dataset.

### Key Analysis Performed

* Analyzed monthly and yearly sales trends to identify seasonality and revenue fluctuations.
* Identified the **top 10 products by revenue and profit margin**.
* Compared sales performance across different **sales channels** such as Wholesale, Distributor, and Export.
* Analyzed **state-wise and regional sales performance** using geographic visualizations.
* Compared revenue and order counts across top-performing states.
* Analyzed top and bottom customers based on revenue contribution.
* Performed **customer segmentation using revenue and profit margin**.
* Studied relationships between numerical variables using a **correlation heatmap**.
* Analyzed Average Order Value (AOV) distribution and order-value patterns.

### Key Insights

* Products **26 and 25** were among the strongest revenue contributors.
* **Wholesale** generated the largest share of total sales, followed by Distributor and Export.
* **California** was the leading state in terms of revenue and order volume.
* The **West region** showed the highest sales performance, while the Northeast had comparatively lower sales.
* Strong correlations were observed between **unit price, cost, revenue, and profit**.
* Sales showed noticeable seasonal fluctuations, highlighting opportunities for targeted seasonal promotions.
* Revenue was concentrated among a smaller group of high-value customers.

### Recommendations

* Introduce targeted promotions during low-performing months.
* Focus on high-performing products while reviewing underperforming SKUs.
* Expand high-margin Export opportunities while maintaining Wholesale volume.
* Replicate successful strategies from high-performing states such as California.
* Monitor customer and order-level profit margins to identify pricing and cost-optimization opportunities.

### Project Outcome

The project delivers an **interactive Power BI dashboard** that enables stakeholders to explore sales performance by time, product, customer, channel, state, and region. The analysis provides actionable insights for **sales strategy, regional investment, product optimization, customer management, and operational planning**.
